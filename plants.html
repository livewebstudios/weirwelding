Since its founding in 1961, Weir Welding has grown into a leader in structural steel fabrication, combining over 65 years of experience with a commitment to quality, integrity, and dependability. Today, the company operates three state-of-the-art fabrication facilities in Carlstadt, NJ; Bethlehem, PA; and Saddle Brook, NJ. We are equipped to handle projects of any scale with precision and efficiency.

These facilities span a total of 160,000 square feet and employ over 80 dedicated professionals who are integral to maintaining Weir Welding's reputation for excellence throughout the Tri-State area.
