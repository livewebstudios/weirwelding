What We Do Best

At Weir Welding, we take pride in offering a comprehensive range of structural steel services designed to meet the diverse and challenging demands of our clients. With decades of experience, we specialize in custom steel fabrication tailored to exact specifications. Our expertise extends to steel and deck erection, providing end-to-end solutions that include the supply, installation, and finishing of structural steel, joists, metal decking, stairs, and railings.

Our advanced facilities and state-of-the-art equipment enable us to handle even the most complex and large-scale projects with precision and efficiency. Certified AWS D1.1 welders and a dedicated team of professionals ensure that every component is fabricated and installed to the highest standards of quality and safety. From concept to completion, we are committed to delivering reliable, on-time, and cost-effective solutions.
